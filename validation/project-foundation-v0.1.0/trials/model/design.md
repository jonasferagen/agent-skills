# Design

Revision: 1 · 2026-10-03

The nursery owns seed lots and sampling; the shop owns customer claims. These
boundaries remain proposals. This slice clarifies how sample evidence can inform
claims; it does not approve a claim or adopt a new operating process.

Observed: two seed lots A and B share cultivar Dawn. A sample from A germinated
at 82 percent; B was not tested. A shop draft labels Dawn as 82 percent germination.
The detailed meaning and disagreement cases are owned by
[seed-model.md](seed-model.md).

Under the proposed responsibility split, the nursery explains which lot and sample
a result concerns and supplies the test context and limitations. The shop decides
whether that evidence supports a claim about the particular seed offered, and
owns the claim's wording and scope. The current cultivar-wide draft is not
supported by the observed evidence. Resolving the meaning alone does not make a
lot-wide claim ready for use.

A later disagreeing sample prompts the nursery to review the observations and
their interpretation, and the shop to reconsider any affected draft or existing
claim. A valid sample observation can remain valid while the broader claim becomes
unsupported. The required evidence and unresolved acceptance decision are tracked
in [plan.md](plan.md).

Work remains conceptual. Storage, general history systems and automation are deferred.
