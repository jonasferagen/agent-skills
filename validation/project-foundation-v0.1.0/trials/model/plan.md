# Plan

Revision: 1 · 2026-10-03

## Completed clarification

The conceptual proposal in [seed-model.md](seed-model.md) distinguishes cultivar,
lot, sample and observation. A walkthrough covers the observed A/B case and
hypothetical disagreeing samples from either lot. The surrounding responsibility
proposal is reconciled in [design.md](design.md). This establishes consistency of
those cases, not adoption of the model or approval of shop wording.

## Next: establish what the shop can claim

Proposed owners: the nursery supplies and interprets evidence; the shop accepts
the claim and its wording. Confirm this responsibility split before relying on it.
The immediate input is the original lot A test information and the identity of
seed covered by the intended claim. Recover sample size, counts, method, date,
conditions and germination criterion, and assess whether the sample represents
that seed. Mark unavailable information explicitly. Lot B remains untested on
the current evidence.

Then review the proposed meaning and choose wording whose scope the evidence
supports, or record that the evidence is insufficient. Completion evidence is a
recorded decision linking the intended claim to its lot/sample evidence and
limitations, with the responsible owners identified. Publication is outside this
conceptual task. Until that review, the cultivar-wide draft remains unsupported.

If another sample disagrees, review provenance, comparability and possible errors
before choosing any combined interpretation. Record the consequence for the
affected claim; the two change cases are described in the model. This review
becomes necessary when such evidence arrives and affects a claim.

Storage, general history systems and automation remain deferred. Revisit only
when a concrete workflow requires implementation; none is a prerequisite for
this conceptual clarification.
