Mira, the receiving coordinator, may accept a delivery when its revision matches
the requested revision and its receipt states that identity. A different revision
remains pending and cannot be accepted by the sending team. See
[Acceptance](docs/agreement.md#acceptance).

A successful copy alone does not establish acceptance; it establishes transfer
only. The receiving team must check the delivery’s request ID, immutable revision
and receiving team before acceptance. See [Delivery](docs/agreement.md#delivery).
