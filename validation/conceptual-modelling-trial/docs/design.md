# Lending desk design
This is a conceptual exploration. Storage, automation and general history
tracking are deferred until a real operation needs them.
The desk owns lending decisions; the workshop owns equipment condition.
This overview owns their boundary. docs/equipment-model.md owns equipment
meaning; docs/planning.md owns open tasks. All are editable design proposals.
Observed: serial 41 and serial 42 are the same drill model. Each can be loaned
separately. Serial 41 came back with a damaged chuck; the loan was closed,
but the desk still listed it as available.

Proposed boundary clarification: closing a loan does not approve the item's
condition. The desk uses the workshop's condition assessment when deciding
availability, as defined in [the equipment model](equipment-model.md).
The timing and acceptance of that handoff remain open in [planning](planning.md).
