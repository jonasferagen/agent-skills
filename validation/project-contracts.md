# Project contracts extraction — 2026-10-08

## Scope

Standalone project-contracts 0.1.0 extracts the native 0.5.0 workflow from
project-foundation `bb95633f4e94c00053b6d43a7a92c493a4698636`.
It preserves separate read, apply, design/change and review routes. Contract
shape and placement belong to the consuming project; the legacy browsing schema,
typed relations and foundation format are not required.

## Informed author walkthroughs

These are author reviews of known instructions and synthetic exchanges, not
independent discovery trials or claims of spontaneous selection.

| Request | Route reviewed | Preserved outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain whether receipt means acceptance | Entry point, relevant agreement | Answer from the terms; silence remains unresolved without redesigning the agreement. |
| Assess a generated candidate against a brief | Apply route | Check exact input/brief identity and relevant criteria; tool success alone does not establish acceptance. |
| Define a design-to-implementation handoff | Design reference | State scope, deciding authority, input revision, result and open terms without turning every internal step into an API. |
| Retry an operation after timeout | Design reference, failure/change section | Unknown outcome differs from confirmed failure; reconciliation precedes a potentially duplicating submission. No provider capability is assumed. |
| Review an added field for compatibility | Review → failure/change section when needed | Compare actual consumer expectations and retained records; schema success alone is insufficient. |
| Correct a sentence without changing terms | Ordinary writing route | No full contract review or format migration. |
| Ask for a legal agreement | Outside skill scope | The description excludes legal agreements; this is an operational handoff workflow. |

Synthetic case: a sender supplies candidate C for brief B, revision 2. A review
of C against revision 1 is evidence for that earlier basis, not acceptance against
revision 2. If the agreement has no rule for changed briefs, application reports
the gap; authorized design work may establish the rule. Neither route silently
rewrites the historical review. A timeout in transferring C does not establish
that the receiver failed to retain it.

## Validation and limits

The package and skill-creator validators pass. Design and review references keep
their internal routes; companion skills are named without external package links.
The full source check passed: five checks, four suites, 157 tests and no skips.
`git diff --check` passed. No release tag was created.
No runtime helpers, schemas or live exchanges are added. Independent read/apply
discovery and negative activation remain unverified; a manual review cannot
establish runtime compliance or reliable activation.

The reusable finding is that successful execution, acceptance and downstream
adoption are separate claims. The agreement needs only the distinctions that
affect its participants, with missing terms left visible during interpretation.
