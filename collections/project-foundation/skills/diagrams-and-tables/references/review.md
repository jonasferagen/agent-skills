# Review the meaning of a view

Use this when the user asks whether an existing diagram or table accurately
represents its subject. Bound the review to the relevant question and revision.
Read the view's caption and legend, then the applicable family reading notes if
notation needs interpretation. Construction references are unnecessary unless
the task includes changing the view.

Compare claims with their authoritative source: the model for intended
relationships, implementation/configuration for observed behavior, and decisions
for adopted responsibility. A proposed diagram need not match deployed behavior,
but its status and assumptions must be clear. An unlabeled arrow cannot establish
permission, sequence or a required relationship by itself.

For each relevant mismatch, identify the drawn or tabulated claim, source evidence,
reader consequence and proposed correction. Preserve meaningful unknowns; absence
from a deliberately scoped diagram is not automatically a missing system feature.
For tables, check headers, units, footnotes and the meaning of blank values before
calling entries inconsistent. Check both ends and role names of relationships.

A semantic finding does not authorize rewriting the model to fit the picture.
If the task includes correction, route the changed portion through its construction
guide. For a requested visual review use [verification](verification.md): a source
comparison cannot establish whether rendered labels collide or become unreadable.
Report semantic and visual coverage separately.
