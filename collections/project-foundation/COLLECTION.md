# Project foundation

Version: 1.3.1

Start with project instructions and their existing document routes. Use this
entrypoint to choose needed guidance; if the owner and workflow are already known,
follow them directly. Source-maintenance guidance and provenance are needed only
for source changes, upgrades or investigation of a concrete source issue.

When execution exposes a consequential guidance or routing problem, use
[friction capture and repair](references/friction.md). Surface effects on the
current task promptly and continue unaffected work. Successful work needs no
capture pass.

Load only the route needed by the question. For reading or applying an existing
document, use its authoritative owner, relevant terms and available evidence.
Use [status and evidence](references/status-and-evidence.md) to distinguish
unknowns, proposals, adopted obligations and verified behavior. A focused answer or literal wording edit needs no authoring
workflow. A change to meaning uses its owning workflow.

Use [foundation authority](references/foundation-authority.md) when establishing
or reconciling decisions, resolving conflicting authority, or assessing changed
scope or circumstances. A narrow task within known constraints does not require
loading that reference. Follow declared inheritance through
[foundation format](references/foundation-format.md) when the answer depends on
it; unavailable owners or unknown terms do not grant permission or waive obligations.

For authored or restructured explanations, use
[shared authoring guidance](references/authoring.md).

Bootstrap owns foundation scaffolding and its templates; project-docs owns view
and process templates.
Reusing an installed documentation template needs no installation workflow.
The [document-home map](references/document-homes.md) owns managed types and their
default templates, including architecture, models, processes and handoff contracts.
Routine use of an installed collection needs neither setup nor every skill loaded.
